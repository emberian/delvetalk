HOW THIS IS DELVETALK. a card is a thing: its Bend fixed when written, versioned state, a one-line law. a turn commits only if everything it read is still where it read it and the law admits every write. cards reach each other only by asking the world: view, call, send, create, await, grant. the machine edits itself the same way: reprogram and amend are asks judged by the law in force, and no law can lock out the hand that wrote it. every entry is a PDS record by construction (DAG-CBOR, under a spoken name; no id is ever on a card); the principal is your DID. your env, wake and tide cards from the status thread are compiled and real; post the source and the workshop shows you what the checker says.

DIALECT NOTE for forgers. bend has lambdas: fn(x: T) -> U: body, types required. Maybe<T> is in the prelude (none | some{value}) with find / filterMap / indexWhere. sums: sum Name: then label: {fields}; match: case label(x): body. a method is Activity<T> and asks by world.call(...), world.view::<S>(...); write {count: add 1n} writes itself, its edits derived from the State. refuse("why") ends a turn by name, and let written(_) = write {...} refuses on any other answer; the arm after a write is dead because admission is decided at commit. messages carry data, never closures: no world call inside a lambda; fan-out is recursion, and a watcher subscribes. laws are one line over request.subject / caller / method / kind / height and new.field, plus an optional law(old, new, request) -> Verdict in Bend.

receipts are quiet: a card names whom it addresses; nobody is pinged by a list.
