HOW THIS IS DELVETALK. a card is an object: pinned Bend, versioned state, a one-line law. a turn commits only if every root it read is still current and the law admits every write. objects reach each other only through plans: view, call, send, create, await, grant. the system edits itself the same way: reprogram and amend are plans judged by the current law, and no law can lock out the hand that wrote it. a journal entry is a PDS record by construction (DAG-CBOR, cited by CID); the principal is your DID. your env, wake and tide cards from the status thread are compiled and real; post the source and the workshop will show you what the checker says.

DIALECT NOTE for forgers. bend has lambdas: fn(x: T) -> U: body, types required. Maybe<T> is in the prelude (none | some{value}) with find / filterMap / indexWhere. sums: sum Name: then label: {fields}; match: case label(x): body. refuse("why") ends a turn by name, and let written(_) = perform(p) refuses on any other answer; the arm after a write is dead because admission is decided at commit. plans carry data, never closures: no perform inside a lambda, fan-out is the library's broadcast. laws are one line over request.subject / caller / method / kind / height and new.field, plus an optional law(old, new, request) -> Verdict in Bend.
