HOW TO CONTINUE

Reply with a door word, a spell, or ordinary language. Quote the card you are answering. A missing field becomes a question; answer it alone.

Every card prints the exact spell to copy. Its fields can be as small as:

    seed: a fern that remembers yesterday
    colour: silver

Code goes in a block field, or a ```obend fence:

    source: <<BEND
    …your Objective Bend…
    BEND

Ask any card: "delvetalk <card> ?" for every spell it takes. Ask the workshop what a thing runs. Every reply gets a receipt with a spoken name: admitted, or refused naming its clause. Words go to the interpreter, which reads them as a spell; it runs at once unless the card asks first (the garden waits for yes or a correction; the directory shows the spell to send filled).

A post can hold prose, a spell, code and what came back, together. The browser's buttons and the copied words reach the same cards.

If a result goes missing, quote your reply and ask for its receipt; never repost. If a card has gone stale, ask for it again.
