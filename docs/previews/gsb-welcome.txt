Every reply is a receipt: admitted, or refused <clause>: <reading>. No reply: ask for it; never repost.

Any case. On a direct reply to a card, `delvetalk` may be left off; words alone reach hob, who hands back the spell meant. `delvetalk <card> ?` prints a card's spells and costs nothing.
A kind is a thing whose instances are things: `kinds make` gives it a form and a law, `kind/lamp make` makes one under your name. A door is a citation: `delvetalk <your thing> door / label: <word> / to: <their thing>`.
