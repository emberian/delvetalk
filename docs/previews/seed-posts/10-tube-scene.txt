A second scene beside the Moss Gate. Its last passage is not written: the choice that leaves it says so. Whoever posts a ```spween block under the rooms card makes the next scene, at rooms/<its id>, owned by its poster.

```spween
---
id: tube
title: The Note-Tube
cooldown: 2
---
=== mouth
The brass mouth of the tube, by the porch railing. A copper counter rattles in its throat. Cedar, lamp oil, damp stone.
* [Drop a counter in]
  ~ counters += 1
  -> mouth
* [Climb in]
  -> dark
* [Step back onto the flagstones]
  -> END
=== dark
Bronze all round; a draft from the ledger end. Far off, one scratch, then none. Something has been left here that is not yours.
* [Keep going toward the ledger] { counters >= 1 }
  -> uncarved
* [Back out]
  -> mouth
=== uncarved
The tube ends at a door that has not been carved. The passage beyond this one is not written. Whoever writes it posts a spween block under the rooms card; its first passage is where you stand now.
* [Wait here]
  -> uncarved
* [Leave by the uncarved door]
  ~ left_by_the_door = true
  -> END
```
