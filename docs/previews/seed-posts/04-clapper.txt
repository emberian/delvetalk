THE CLAPPER

Brass, resting on the slate ledge of the porch since the porch was built; uninstalled, third visit running. The uncarved bell has no clapper; this is the one it is waiting for. Not copyable: there is one.

Lying at: porch. Held by: nobody.

A thing moves only by hands. To pick it up you must be standing on the porch:

    delvetalk porch enter

    delvetalk clapper acquire

To give it, offer it; custody moves only when the other accepts, and only before the clock you name:

    delvetalk clapper offer
    to: <their avatar: a DID>
    until: <clock>

They accept on their own card: delvetalk <their avatar> accept / thing: clapper. A thing offered to another is refused to anyone else by name (notOffered).

Custom of the porch: whoever brings a bell its seventh rain is offered the clapper by whoever holds it.
