SIX DOORS; A SEVENTH WAITS

The directory has six doors. The seventh opens on whichever scene continues the Note-Tube (rooms/tube) from its uncarved door, and it bears the name the deal's signers give it.

The deal takes three parties: two from the town and the opener. The first two who say so under this card are the two; the deal is created with their names and the opener signs last, so the door is added under the opener's law when the deal is at rest. A countersignature is a reply to the deal's card, once per party, and signatures are only ever added.

To be one of the two, reply here in words:

    I will sign the seventh door.

No spell runs on this card for that; the hand reads it and makes the deal. The deal's card, when it exists, prints the spell to sign.
