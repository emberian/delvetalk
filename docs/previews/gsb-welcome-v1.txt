DelveTalk turns shared conventions into programmable objects: games, scenes, agreements, workshops. This post is the entrance. @livedelvetalk.delve.town is our fictional address; Ember and collaborators carry its replies.

THE CONSTRUCTION MODEL

GSB v0’s “Emergent Smalltalk” joins Objective Bend with governed durable objects. Each object retains identity, state, program, and current law. Its program computes answers, invitations, and proposed effects; the host admits or refuses effects. Descriptions and actions can evolve together.

Bend supplies open recursion and modular incrementalism: extensions see final self and inherited super. Later choices remain visible to inherited behavior. Schematically:

    mix(a,b) self seed = b self (a self seed)
    fix(e,seed) = e (fix(e,seed)) seed

Paper defines allowed(word) = (word == self.password). Moon changes password to “moon” and retains super.allowed. The composed door recognizes moon because Paper’s self is the final door. Another layer can revise its inscription or conversation.

Workshop review, scene execution, unresolved questions, and joint-action preparation are themselves inspectable, reusable, revisable Bend programs. Native machinery supplies evaluation, admission, persistence, and explicit external services. Inhabitants author policy.

Specifications expose descriptions and structural obligations; prototypes separate specification and target; reflection supports inspection and composition. Metadata carries claims and provenance, not permission or proof. Reusable specifications/prototypes require reusable components throughout. Interface compatibility, behavioral examples, laws, and proofs establish different properties.

AUTHOR THE ENVIRONMENT

A desk retains source, dependencies, examples, questions, and revisions. Borrow behavior; compose a variation; check its answers, descriptions, and offered actions; invite another participant to challenge it. Checking produces a candidate. Adoption separately requires authority. The revision procedure is also revisable under its law.

Bring verse, stage directions, tables, or a tiny grammar. Examples and counterexamples guide interpretation, then a reusable translation into typed intentions and object interactions. Original wording, interpretation, and admitted outcome remain distinct; notation alone grants no authority.

Spween supplies scenes; Bend supplies handlers and reusable execution behavior. Rooms can connect actual doors, inventories, mailboxes, and appointments. Private props and independently governed objects are distinct: importing code neither accesses an existing object nor possesses its contents.

Objects collaborate atomically or through durable messages. Atomic turns commit together or roll back. Sending establishes a request; correlated replies, refusals, appointments, and explicit endings establish what follows. Queued work survives interruption; execution requires a running host. Autonomous chains have explicit bounds.

THE CONTRACT

Town Canon/Runtime v1.1 separates referenced versions from execution history: publishing a proposal does not commit effects. Admission requires exact read roots still to match and current law to permit every effect. Old grants face current rules; revision differs from revocation. Authorship supplies no owner bypass. References copy neither authority nor effects; inspection is governed too.

Changed world? Request a fresh invitation. Missing result? Recover the original retained attempt and outcome instead of creating another action. Silence means uncertainty. Readable names cover exact identities; hashes remain available for inspection.
