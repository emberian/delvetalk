# An inhabited forge, through posts

**A maker writes a door; a visitor uses it; the maker revises its behavior and inscription.** Ordinary replies work through [retained operator interpretation](../profiles/MANUAL-INTAKE.md); [literal spells](../profiles/TOWN.md) are optional.

The [forge package](../protocols/town-forge/README.md) supplies governed door/desk factories. Actual makers receive door programming/law authority and desk submission/adoption; configured visitors knock, and the compiler reports. Names, copied source and passing examples grant nothing.

Desks retain exact `target`, `source`, `scenarios` strings and the verified submitter. `objective-bend-spell@1` compiles real textual Bend through Lean: extensions, `self`, `super`, composition and `fix` define behavior and views. Textual examples name test principals, inputs and expected observations; receiving uses the verified reply author. This initial binding is stateless; migration is explicitly `{}`. Every revision/challenge needs a fresh one-shot desk. Pending panels expose source/examples against the captured root; source views bind the configured compiled runtime.

The [compiler queue](../profiles/COMPILER-QUEUE.md) runs admitted source/examples through the selected host. Reports retain receipts, observed differences and engine pins; fixture assertions are runner comparisons, not general proofs. [Authoring follow-ups](../scripts/town_authoring.py) bind the original reply and exact job, preserve source/results, and never rewrite its submission response. Pending work, failed assertions, timeout and uncertain transport remain distinct; retries preserve identity.

Adoption releases the ready desk and reprograms the exact target from its result in **one transaction**. Both current laws/roots must pass. A lone release installs nothing; stale cards refuse. Confirmed adoption yields the revised door card.

[Acceptance journey](../conformance/test_town_forge_journey.py): allocate through bound factory cards; submit/compile/adopt; let another participant use the door; challenge v1 without changing it; revise to v2; refuse stale adoption; adopt afresh and use the changed behavior. Tests use actual Lean with mocked PDS transport. They establish neither live publication nor a new semantic endpoint.
