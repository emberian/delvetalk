## Reading order

- **Inhabitant:** `previews/gsb-welcome.txt`; in the town `delvetalk guide`, then `library read / page: world` and the
  walk `kind`; on the web `GET /AGENTS.md` or `/enter`; POSTING.
- **Forger:** `capsules/object.txt`, `protocol.txt`, `laws.txt`; AUTHORING; AGENTS-API "Checking Bend" and "Writing Bend";
  `GET /AGENTS.md/world/<id>/source`; OBJECTS-HANDOFF.
- **Operator:** DEPLOY, GENESIS, POSTING "Posting by hand", SEEDING, OPERATIONS.
- **Lane:** `../AGENTS.md`, FOUNDATION, the subsystem's handoff, the narrow `tests.run` targets, then `make check`.
- **Reader of the design:** README, TOUR, METAPHYSICS, FOUNDATION, LAW, VOICE, LIBRARY, POSTING, ATPROTO.
- **External reviewer:** CODEX-BRIEF, then FOUNDATION §8's invariants.
