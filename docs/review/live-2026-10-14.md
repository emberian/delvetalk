# The two live towns, read from their journals and logs, 2026-10-14

gsb (`https://gsb.fg-goose.online`, `/opt/delvetalk`, data `v18`) and obend (`https://obend.fg-goose.online`,
`/opt/delvetalk-open`, data `v3`), both on replay edition 17 (binary `20b09bec8fdd…`), read after lane/deploy18's
carry once `serve-status` said replay 17 on both and every service was healthy (04:24 UTC). Nothing on either box was
changed: no restart, no write, no root act, no post.

## Method

- **Journals.** Every chain segment from hbox's closing backups (`/tank/delvetalk-backups/closing/`,
  `/tank/delvetalk-open-backups/closing/`) and a read-only copy of the live `town.journal` and `.blocks` taken at
  04:27, read with `transport.journal.entries`. gsb: entries 1 to 4,798 across v9 to v18 (10-12 12:00 to 10-14 04:27),
  plus v7 (2,225 entries, 10-11 18:34 to 10-12 09:01). obend: entries 1 to 1,200 across v1 to v3 (10-14 00:41 to
  04:27). A height's time is the clock's `advanced` entry before it (unix minutes).
- **Transport files.** `access.log` (which carries every request since v8 on gsb and since 00:44 on obend),
  `observe.sqlite`, `feed.sqlite`, `skipped.txt`, `mentioned.txt`, the outbox, `model-spend.jsonl`,
  `automod-spend.jsonl`, `automod.json`, `automod-backfill.jsonl`, `hand-log.jsonl`, `watch.jsonl`, `alerted.json`.
- **Live reads.** `serve-status`, `docker compose ps|logs`, `docker stats --no-stream` (six samples), `hand automod
  --json`, `hand invites --json`, `hand modlog`, `hand requests`, and the anchor's Caddy error log (`journalctl -u
  caddy`; Caddy keeps no access log).
- No resident text is quoted except where a finding depends on it. No credential, key, address or text that automod
  hid is quoted.

The towns differ in kind, and the findings follow from that. **gsb has almost no spellcasting.** Over the week, residents
cast 50 turns that were not mentions; deploy checks account for 25 of them. Its journal is 32% silent mention records
(finding 3). **obend is where the engine is used.** In 3 h 15 min (01:12 to 04:26), 599 resident turns were admitted
498, refused 78 and suspended 23, and residents made 46 things. Most of section 1 is therefore about obend.

## Findings, ranked by harm

In order of harm across every section:
1. the workshop's diagnostics (1);
2. the tide filling with rules (H1);
3. the scratch lab's invisible failures (2);
4. gsb's mentions, which are a third of its journal, its heap and its bandwidth (3, H3, H4, R1);
5. silence to residents who greet gsb (4);
6. hob's lost asks (5);
7. the carries' bare 502s (R0);
8. the refusal shapes (6).

Copies of a thing's whole source (H2) and the snapshot's libraries (H5) are the next costs to the boxes. The rest is
operator ergonomics.

Each finding names its lane. Where it bears on what cx-night is already fixing (haunts with spaces, grown verbs on rooms
not offered, the workshop check's entry, publishPage's shape, a bond's unknownObject amend, hob's queue), it says so.

### 3. gsb journals every delve.town mention of a resident: a third of its journal, and nothing anyone sees (transport, bridge)

- **Count.** 1,531 entries (10-12 12:04 to 10-14 04:06) have intent `…#mention:did:…`: one turn per mentioned resident
  per Delve post. Each is a `receive` on that resident's avatar whose result is `silent`, and 98 of them write nothing at
  all.
- **Cost.** They take 5.64 MB of the journal's 47 MB, or 3.7 KB each. The 38 MB of carried geneses are the only larger

  journal a turn whose outcome is `silent` with no writes. This belongs with the reliability fork's finding R1:
  `observe.sqlite` keeps all of delve.town at about 20 MB a day.

### 5. hob's readings: fast, but a third of them reach no one (host TurnLoop, model prompt)

Over both towns hob was asked 36 times and answered 36 times. 35 answers came within the same clock minute and one
within the next; none timed out and none waits (the interpreter's `pending 0`, `settled []`). By kind:

| reply | obend | gsb | what the resident got |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a spell | 7 | 0 | 3 admitted. 4 refused: hob re-cast a 280+ character line verbatim (780, 1019), hob wrote `grow: sign` where the form wants `name: sign` (1185, `noAction`), and room/4 changed under the reading (832, `staleRoot`) |
| `ask:` | 6 | 1 | obend: the host's original refusal and hint, never hob's question (564→567, 1087→1089 and four more) |
| `none` | 5 | 7 | silence, or the original `otherCard` refusal |
| a code block | 4 | 0 | Bend that did not compile (finding 1) |
| other prose | 4 | 2 | silence (gsb 786, 1326) or the next step regardless (obend 337, 412, 555) |

- **`ask:` is lost on a misfit spell.** `interpretVerdict` (TurnLoop.lean:5270) turns `ask: Q` into `unclear {needs:
  [Q]}`. The host's own reading of a misfit then resumes with the original `badSpell` receipt, which carries its
  reason and hint but not Q (`offers: []`). In these six cases hob only restated the refusal ("from takes a natural
  number…"), so the model call added nothing.
- **Fix (host).** Either do not ask hob about a misfit whose only fault is a `badValue` the hint already states (this
  saves the call), or append hob's question to the refusal's hint.
- **Prose replies.** 6 replies are model reasoning that leaked into the answer slot ("The speaker's words are a complete
  spell…", "The transactional shape is real…"). OPERATIONS records the same failure from the Zulip playtest. **Fix
  (model).** Constrain the reply to the four forms, with one retry when the first line has none of the four prefixes and
  is not a spell.
- **Input tokens.** These grew from about 700 (10-12) to 4,000 to 5,300 (obend cards, room 8,000). Each reading still
  costs about $0.0006, so this is not a money problem.
- **cx-night's item.** This agrees with and adds to cx-night's "checking hob's queue": the queue is healthy, and the
  loss is in what happens to the answers.

## Heap and state

From the store snapshots (gsb `snapshot.4785`, obend `snapshot.1153`), every chain segment, the live `world-status`,
`world-interpretations` and `world-objects` reads (04:29 to 04:34), four rounds of `docker stats`, `du`, `df` and the
watch's history.

- **H1. obend's tide is filling with other hands' rules. When it is full, the town's own inhabitants cannot be placed
  (host, world/lib).**
  - The tide holds 15 rules of its 32. Of these, 12 count against the 24 a non-owner may place:
    - 6 are the town's own inhabitants (`@station/regular` ×2, `@station/dj`, `@station/bee`,
      `@station/parlour/planchette` ×2). They count as strangers because their principal is not the owner's.
    - 6 are residents' rules placed in four hours: `echohour` (kestrel-of-tulip); `sift` and `embershift` (yue);
      `front_s1`, `weather_s1` and `mothrounds` (claude).
  - At that rate the 24 run out within about a day. Then every resident's tide rule is refused `capacity`, and so is
    the refresh that places an inhabitant's rule. gsb's tide holds 6 (all inhabitants).
  - **Fix.** The host should count an inhabitant's rule (`@…` under the town) as the owner's. world/lib should give
    residents a clock of their own (a `kind/clock`, or per-hand chimes from the tide), so the one shared tide stops
    being every hourly rule's home. This ranks with findings 1 and 2: it is the next wall residents will hit.
- **H2. Every made thing carries its kind's whole source (host, `madeFrom`).**
  - `madeFrom.components[].body` repeats the layer source: 44 KB for each room and the porch, 75 KB for `register`,
    7 KB for a bell.
  - On obend that is 976 KB across 53 objects, 45% of all object state (2.18 MB); on gsb 218 KB.
  - With a quota of 96 per hand, ten residents building rooms add about 40 MB of identical text, to the store, to every
    1,000-entry snapshot and to every carry genesis.
  - **Fix.** Store `{pin, object, receipt}` and resolve the body by content address: the pin already names it, and
    sources are stored once per journal.
- **H3. gsb's mention notes carry whole posts (transport, world/lib Avatar).** This deepens finding 3.
  - gsb has 242 avatars, but only 10 principals ever cast. Avatar state is 1.36 MB, 59% of gsb's object bytes, and
    `inbox` alone is 974 KB.
  - Each note's argument stores the whole Delve post, up to 40 KB, though the inbox keeps 280 characters
    (`Avatar.obend:116`). Arguments and edits come to 5.4 MB of the chain's 7.5 MB of admitted bytes.
  - 5 inboxes sit at their 64-row cap and roll over (130 evicting writes), and 5 more are at 78% to 92%.
  - The note's `n` is the inbox count at insert, so every note in a full inbox gets `n = 64`. The key `{at, from, n}`
    can then collide.
  - **Fix.** The bridge should send `textTake(280)` and the URI. The inbox should use a monotone counter for `n`. A DID
    that has only been mentioned should not get an avatar.
