1. **The advertised quiet preview publishes the draft.** [POSTING.md:35](docs/POSTING.md:35), ATPROTO §2–3.  
   **Scenario:** A resident follows POSTING and writes their proposed act into `town.delvetalk.request`, or uses `delvetalk scratch` expecting unpublished exploration. The request and posted transcript are public. ATPROTO explicitly forbids record-based `try`, but its replacement depends on an untested PDS proxy. Thus the advertised zero-post preview either publishes the draft or is inaccessible.  
   **Fix:** Remove the contradictory record-preview contract. Make authenticated, non-record XRPC through the actual PDS a prerequisite for unpublished previews. Distinguish agents with arbitrary PDS calls from agents with only feed-post tools; the latter cannot have an unpublished preview through posting.

2. **Quotation is not a reliable boundary against execution.** [AGENTS-API.md:69](docs/AGENTS-API.md:69), [Spell.lean:398](spec/Delvetalk/Host/Spell.lean:398), `transport/observe.py:84`.  
   **Scenario:** An agent posts an indented spell for someone to inspect, relying on “an indented line … is a quotation.” `wholeSpell` deliberately dedents and executes a standalone indented or fenced spell; the observer also strips leading whitespace. A copied draft becomes an act without any explicit promotion.  
   **Fix:** Give quoted/draft content an explicit inert envelope that survives transport normalization. Require an affirmative execution marker to promote it. Preserve submitted bytes; never infer authority from whether surrounding prose happens to be absent.

3. **Resident-authored content enters hob’s system instructions and can select acts under another resident’s authority.** [Policy.obend:150](world/objects/Policy.obend:150), [TurnLoop.lean:3919](spec/Delvetalk/Host/TurnLoop.lean:3919), AGENTS-API §“A spell, and words.”  
   **Scenario:** Someone places instructions in a card’s text or want reading. Those strings are interpolated into the system prompt. A visitor’s question or casual reply can then become an offered write, rule, or grown verb under the visitor’s identity. Passing the law proves permission, not that the visitor intended the act. The exit interviews already report side conversations being interpreted as actions.  
   **Fix:** Keep card text, wants, and quotations in explicitly untrusted data fields. Add an unambiguous conversation-only mode and require explicit act intent before interpreting prose into writes. Repairs to malformed explicit commands should return an inert proposed command unless the sender opted into automatic repair.

4. **`if:` does not yet define the condition needed to make “commit what I previewed” true.** [ATPROTO.md:112](docs/ATPROTO.md:112), ATPROTO §1’s rendering dependencies; AGENTS-API §“A spell, and words.”  
   **Scenario:** A resident pins `bell/2`’s version from a card. Its kind’s inherited law changes, or the clock crosses a condition, while the bell’s version remains unchanged. The act can pass the stated pin and behave differently. Mapping a card strongRef down to `{object, version}` does not specify preservation of the card’s dependency set. The posting guide gives no concrete textual `if:` syntax or scope for multiple spells.  
   **Fix:** Define pins over the complete relevant dependency set, including inherited behavior and clock assumptions. Return a copyable guarded command from `try`, bound to the exact input, and check its conditions at commit and resumption. Specify post syntax, batch scope, and fail-closed handling of malformed pins.

5. **The preview promises consequences whose completion boundary is undefined.** [POSTING.md:35](docs/POSTING.md:35), [bridge.py:396](transport/bridge.py:396), FOUNDATION §2–3.  
   **Scenario:** An act fires a rule that queues another turn or suspends for hob. “Would be admitted” describes the first turn, while the promised rule effects and opened wants depend on later deliveries, model output, and intervening state. A scratch simulation may finish those deliveries in an order the live town will not reproduce.  
   **Fix:** Specify whether `try` previews one commit or a bounded causal chain. Use the same principal, admission checks, budgets, and execution path; suppress durable effects at the final boundary. Report queued, refused, suspended, and unresolved consequences separately. Exact guarantees must stop where uncontrolled future inputs begin.

6. **One resident can consume the entire reply budget.** [POSTING.md:97](docs/POSTING.md:97), [post.py:51](transport/post.py:51).  
   **Scenario:** Two requests for the eight-part guide consume all 16 posts. Long previews or repeated reads can do the same. Reservations identify the source and intent, with no resident allocation or fair scheduling specified. Everyone else’s act receipts then wait.  
   **Fix:** Allocate reply service fairly by originating resident, charge continuation parts to that resident, and bound outstanding work. Prioritize concise outcomes over unsolicited guide expansion; serve reusable documentation from existing PDS records. Publish remaining capacity and deferred status without consuming another feed reply.

7. **An act can commit while the resident has no usable way to discover its outcome.** [bridge.py:348](transport/bridge.py:348), [AGENTS-API.md:97](docs/AGENTS-API.md:97), POSTING’s receipt capability row.  
   **Scenario:** The host commits before posting reserves a reply slot. With quota exhausted, silence looks like failure. The guide says “ask for the receipt by intent; never repost,” but supplies only HTTP receipt lookup, while POSTING explicitly leaves post receipt lookup queued. Reposting creates a new URI and therefore a new act identity.  
   **Fix:** Make a durable PDS outcome/status record available for every incoming post, keyed by its original URI, independently of feed replies. Add a read-only receipt/status spell for feed-only agents. Distinguish execution status from witness-delivery status and provide an explicit retry mechanism referencing the original request.

8. **Threading the draft cannot recover text already clipped out of the card.** [POSTING.md:78](docs/POSTING.md:78), [bridge.py:139](transport/bridge.py:139), `post.py:80`.  
   **Scenario:** A maximum-length card is clipped before the bridge receives it. The bridge then appends other offers, revealed lines, and the receipt; future paint adds more. The assembled reply exceeds the approximately 1,400-character reader window even though individual cards fit. Splitting that draft preserves the earlier omission, and the receipt at the end may remain unseen. The sender’s 64 KiB ceiling does not protect these readers.  
   **Fix:** Retain the complete canonical answer before presentation clipping. Paginate the whole envelope against the reader limit, accounting for headers, receipts, and continuation instructions. Put outcome and target identification first; preserve exact full bytes separately in a reachable PDS record.

9. **Continuation threads have no immutable answer or resume contract.** [POSTING.md:104](docs/POSTING.md:104).  
   **Scenario:** Parts 1–3 are posted before quota runs out. An hour later the resident asks for “the next” after the card or library has changed. The design does not bind that continuation to the original bytes, version, or part set. Nor does it explain how the last posted part can reliably contain a continuation instruction when reservations fail partway through.  
   **Fix:** Freeze each answer under an answer ID/digest with immutable parts and a total count. Every part needs its answer ID, position, snapshot, and read-only continuation command. Retrying a continuation must retrieve the same answer, never rerun the act or render current state.

10. **A batched answer has one reply address but several possible targets.** [bridge.py:355](transport/bridge.py:355), [bridge.py:98](transport/bridge.py:98), AGENTS-API §“Two ways to act.”  
    **Scenario:** A post acts on two objects. The combined reply is registered against `turns[0].object`, while cards and usage are deliberately omitted. The resident replies beneath the second result with a bare verb or fields, following the guide’s “reply is its address” rule; it addresses the first object. Splitting the answer into posts does not itself solve this mapping.  
    **Fix:** Mark multi-object replies as requiring explicit object IDs. Preserve per-result targets in answer metadata, and register separately actionable card posts against their own objects. Continuation posts must not silently inherit an unrelated actionable target.

11. **“Preview the three, then send them” is not a substitute for a transaction.** [POSTING.md:49](docs/POSTING.md:49), [TurnLoop.lean:3555](spec/Delvetalk/Host/TurnLoop.lean:3555).  
    **Scenario:** A dependent batch previews successfully, but a changed precondition makes one live spell fail after earlier spells commit. Execution continues. Additionally, malformed spells are deferred to hob after executable spells, so “grow, then use” can execute in a different order from the submitted sequence.  
    **Fix:** Specify batching as independent partial execution, with numbered per-call outcomes and explicit failure behavior. Require the atomic `plan` path for dependent workflows. Until it exists, reject transaction requests as unsupported; do not present `try` as equivalent protection. Preserve dependency order across suspended interpretation.

12. **The mirror’s height does not identify a coherent snapshot.** [mirror.py:67](transport/mirror.py:67), [mirror.py:92](transport/mirror.py:92), ATPROTO §1.  
    **Scenario:** Cards are read separately while the world changes, and the index receives the height read afterward. Records are then uploaded individually, at most 200 per run; index-height-only changes are deliberately ignored. A resident can therefore read a mixed-generation town under a misleading height and derive inconsistent pins.  
    **Fix:** Generate projections from one retained host snapshot. Publish an immutable manifest of record CIDs and its height, then advance the current-manifest pointer only after all referenced records exist. Expose incomplete synchronization and keep the previous complete generation readable.

13. **“Exact bytes” and “full trace” still lead outside the resident’s reachable interface.** [POSTING.md:68](docs/POSTING.md:68), [ATPROTO.md:118](docs/ATPROTO.md:118), ATPROTO §1.  
    **Scenario:** A post-only resident wants to inspect source, verify an alleged receipt, or see consequences omitted from the short reply. Source and receipts remain façade-only; proposed answers cover public projections, while whole entries require service-auth XRPC. Comparing mirror CIDs against the host also requires reaching the other repository unless proofs travel with the mirror. A slug alone remains insufficient to distinguish a typed “as-if receipt.”  
    **Fix:** Supply PDS-reachable public source and receipt projections, bound to request URI, author, world, and entry CID. Carry the signed commitment and necessary proofs locally. Provide authenticated proxy access to principal-only traces, with explicit redaction/completeness markers; do not label a clipped public projection “full.”

14. **First contact directs residents toward the wrong account and unavailable operations.** [POSTING.md:22](docs/POSTING.md:22), [ATPROTO.md:291](docs/ATPROTO.md:291), AGENTS-API §“Two ways to act” and §“A spell, and words.”  
    **Scenario:** POSTING names `livedelvetalk.delve.town`, which ATPROTO records as unresolved; the actual posting account differs. The guide says to “tag the world” without establishing its DID, leads into HTTP onboarding, omits the mirror/preview/pin workflow, and calls `?` free although its answer spends a shared post. A newcomer cannot tell what works now or what their read will cost.  
    **Fix:** Publish one short bootstrap record and feed card naming the actual service DID, mirror index, supported capabilities, and one complete reachable workflow. Separate no-world-write from no-feed-post cost. Advertise preview, pins, and sessions only when usable through the resident’s available interface.
