# Small foundations for inhabited worlds

Design conventions; ordinary protocols, current law, exact roots and retained
receipts remain the execution boundary.

1. **Identify before describing.** World/object references differ from names,
   routes and snapshots. Copying references grants neither custody nor authority.
   Foreign resolution needs an explicit route and trust decision. Prefer specific
   `context`, `about`, `replyTo` relationships over universal inheritance.
2. **Separate presence and authority.** Principals, entities, declared locations
   and connections differ. Spween holds a shared session; the
   [commons registry](../protocols/commons/README.md) admits individual movement
   within one object. Offered leave/enter calls cannot force callers to submit
   both legs.
3. **Separate conversation and work status.** Prose proves no described event.
   Explicit [claim/submission/review](../protocols/work-ticket/README.md) supports
   resumption without continuous conversation; it supplies no scheduler or delivery
   guarantee.
4. **Version meaning, syntax and presentation independently.** Unknown required
   semantics stop automation while allowing raw inspection. Family names are
   metadata; retained exact programs and roots bind behavior. Incompatible meaning
   needs a new version.
5. **Adopt notation explicitly.** Preserve source, reviewed adapter and lowering.
   Compilation grants no installation rights. Prose cannot select parsers, refresh
   saved roots or confer roles.

[Transactions](../profiles/TransactionsCore.lean) accept explicit input or earlier
results. `inputFrom` supplies data with host-authenticated origin from an earlier admitted
invocation; it grants no authority. A protocol can require a particular immediately
preceding producer. Atomicity covers the submitted batch, not an assumed complete
workflow; retained-event delivery remains separate.

Precedents inspected 2026-10-08:
[LambdaMOO movement](https://www.hayseed.net/MOO/manuals/ProgrammersManual.html)
separates location, permissions and hooks;
[ActivityStreams](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitystreams-vocabulary/#properties)
distinguishes context/replies/relationships;
[RFC 9110 §12](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-12)
separates representation negotiation from semantics. These inform the conventions;
they are not adopted execution models or deployment evidence.
