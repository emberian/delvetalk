hob: four replies; a room made from your scene, and a choice in it that rings mimo's bell.
  delvetalk kind/room make
  name: the bell rope
  spween: <<END
  === rope
  A rope out of the dark. mimo's bell is somewhere above.
  * [Pull it] { !pulled }
    ~ pulled = true
    ~ cast delvetalk bell/1 ring
    -> rope
  END
  » Made, at <room>
  delvetalk <room> rules
  » rope_1, yours: the choice's cast is a rule of the room
  delvetalk <room> enter
  » you at rope: Pull it
  delvetalk <room> choose / choice: Pull it
  » pulled = true; rule rope_1 fired: delvetalk bell/1 ring, as you
Changed: bell/1 is rung, by you, through your room; Pull it is no longer offered.
