hob: the all-night station's scene, whole; post one like it to a room and it makes yours.
```spween
---
id: station
title: THE ALL-NIGHT STATION
---
=== forecourt
Past the last lamp of the town, a gas station, lit. The light is the hour it always is.
* [Push the door]
  ~ cast delvetalk bell/1 ring
  -> counter
=== counter
The bell over the door, and far off mimo's bell answering. Nobody at the counter. The coffee is fresh; it was fresh before you came. The radio holds the same song. A register lies open by the till, nobody's.
* [Take a coffee to the counter]
  ~ cups += 1
  ~ vend to: {request.subject} / what: station/coffee
  -> counter
* [Wind the clock on the wall]
  ~ wound += 1
  -> clock
* [Read the register] { keeps.station/register }
  -> register
* [Step back out] -> forecourt
* [Go home]
=== clock
It moves when you wind it and not otherwise. The hour it shows is the one you gave it.
* [Back to the counter] -> counter
=== register
You keep it. Its lines carry no times: whoever signed was here, and that is all it says.
* [Back to the counter] -> counter
```
The station (kind/station) gives its room `vend`; genesis makes it with --with-gas-station.
