hob: a scene is a room's own story, walked by choosing.
---  optional: id, title, cooldown (clock minutes), requires (min max flags not has)
=== start  a passage; the first is where a reader begins; prose alone is a scene of one
Prose.  what a reader there reads; no passage falls through to the next
* [Label] -> next  a choice; with no -> it ends the visit
* [Label] { gold >= 2, keeps.porch/goblet, "bell/1" wants rain }  a guard: the room's own variables, what the reader keeps or holds, any law condition
  ~ gold += 1  the room's variables: = += -=
  ~ cast delvetalk bell/1 ring  a spell, cast under the room's owner as a rule's then; its rules show it
  ~ ring bell/1  the room's own verb: delvetalk <room> ring / text: bell/1
-> next  outside a choice: a Continue to next
Choices are sticky; once is a guard: { !rang } with ~ rang = true.
A whole one:
delvetalk kind/room make / name: the well / spween: <<END
=== well
A well hums under the square.
* [Drop a coin] { !wished }
  ~ wished = true
  -> deep
* [Walk on]
=== deep
The water answers with a low bell.
* [Ring it back]
  ~ cast delvetalk bell/1 ring
END
A ```spween block posted to a room makes one under it.
