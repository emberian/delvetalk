LAWS, RULES, WANTS: one grammar, three keywords, one line each, in the source or given at creation;
at most 16 lines and 4,096 bytes a text; top-level fields only.
  law NAME "reading": EXPR              refuses at commit; every law must admit the write
  rule NAME "reading": EXPR then SPELL  fires at every commit while EXPR holds, under its placer: a level
  want NAME "reading": EXPR asks SPELL  poses while EXPR holds now, judged at read and at commit; no old
A thing also carries placed lines (delvetalk CARD rule / name / when / then; want / name / when / asks;
32 of each), judged by its law as a request of kind rule; unrule, unwant take one off (placer or owner).
EXPR: A implies B (loosest, right) | A or B | A and B | not A | (A) | ATOM.
ATOMS. TERM == TERM, !=, <, <=, >, >= (!= false when a side reads nothing); TERM ::= TERM + TERM | TERM % INT | REF | INT | "TEXT". TERM in [INT,..],
in new.F (a list), in SIDE.F.COL (SIDE new, old, or "OBJECT": another card's rows, a root).
monotone writeOnce appendOnly unchanged insertOnly (F). COUNTS count(new.F [where COL == TERM]).
changed(TERM): differs across this write, so count(new.lines) == 3 and
changed(count(new.lines)) fires once at the third line where >= 3 fires at every line after;
% 3 == 0 and changed(..) each third. changed(new.marks.who) by request.subject. under "kind/lamp": that kind's law, rules and wants hold here
(every made thing is under its kind, so its owner changes them once for all). "OBJECT" wants NAME.
REF: new.F | old.F (empty at creation) | "OBJECT".F | count(..) | request.subject | caller | height |
turn | pin | method | verb (a grown verb's name; grow on a placing) | kind | clock (world minutes) |
object. kind: 0 the object's own method, 1 reprogram, 2 amend, 3 proposed, 4 rule (a placing of a
rule, a want or a verb). A missing or mistyped value fails closed.
SPELL holes {TERM}: {request.subject} {request.object} {new.maker} {"garden".tended}; me = the rule
owner's avatar. A hole naming nothing is refused when placed. A want reads no old, no changed, no under.
FIRING at most 8 rules a commit, chains 4 deep; one that cannot fire is unfired (hole spell noOwner
quiet ruleDepth rulesPerCommit pending). Each firing is its owner's turn and receipt; rules reads
them back with the last firing.
READING a refusal by clause N says "refused N: <reading>"; under a kind the kind's clause is named.
delvetalk library laws / card: <id> prints every line, following under.
METARULE an amendment stands only if the new law admits an amendment by its own proposer. With no law:
owner: request.kind == 0 or request.kind == rule or request.subject == "<creator>".
PREDICATE (optional) def law(old, new, request) -> Abi.Verdict, pure, 100,000 ticks, after the text.
