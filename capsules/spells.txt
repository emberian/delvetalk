SPELLS as the host reads them (spec/Delvetalk/Host/Spell.lean; Bend reads none; op spell-parse {text, form?}
shows a parse).
LINE delvetalk CARD ACTION. CARD 1-128 bytes of [A-Za-z0-9:/._-], the object-id rule
(Limits.nameAlphabet); ACTION [a-z0-9-]+ or ?. env, wake = your env/<did>, wake/<did>; me = your avatar.
CAST Only a delvetalk line at a line's start casts. An indented one is a quotation (every card prints its
spells indented). A post holding a card's header (✾ , KIND , a line ending Make one:) beside a delvetalk line
quotes that card: badSpell quotation, nothing cast. A post that is one spell block and nothing else
(indented, or in one plain fence) is dedented and cast. Several line-start spells in one post are a turn
each (identity #1..#3), in order; a fourth is refused tooManySpells.
FIELDS On the line: / name: v / name: v. Then name: value lines, value trimmed. Blank, #, > and fence
lines skipped; a line whose name the form lacks, or prose, ends the fields.
BLOCK name: <<DELIM ... a line exactly DELIM. DELIM 1-32 of [A-Z0-9_]; lines joined by newline.
BARE On a reply to a card, `delvetalk` may go: a first line naming one of its actions (plant / seed: x)
is its spell. Any case; a value without its name fills the field its type fits (amber -> colour).
? delvetalk CARD ? -> the card's spells, blanks shown; nothing journaled.
FIT A field is judged by the card's form: text A..B (a text 0..N may be left out); natural in plain digits,
<= 10^9; choice = one of its words; source = Bend, a <<BEND block or the reply's ```obend fence.
MISSING fields: no refusal; the card answers with the spell and its blanks; that line alone completes it.
MISFIT A spell that does not fit is badSpell, a refused turn, intent bound, public {clause, reason,
hint}: otherCard noAction unknownField duplicateField badValue unclosedBlock quotation tooManySpells, and
fixed (a set naming a fixed field). On a card that reads words (its policy names an object: the
commonplace) an otherCard/noAction/unknownField/badValue misfit goes to receive instead, and hob asks.
PROSE receive gets the text: a card with a policy asks hob (spells, ask: a question, card, say: two
sentences, or none; a proposal, never authority); one without hands it on to the commonplace.
REFUSALS. unknownField/badValue: fix that line per the hint, resend. noAction/otherCard: the hint lists
the real spells. unclosedBlock: end with a line that is exactly DELIM. quotation: write the spell alone.
