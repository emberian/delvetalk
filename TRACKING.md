# DelveTalk status

**No v1 has launched. Hosted worlds are disposable previews.** Fresh seeds replace
older previews. A running world's exact admission, durable receipts and uncertain
reply recovery remain contracts. Local qualification does not establish deployment.

**Opening policy:** newcomers may play freely and make participant-owned objects.
Shared behavior changes use proposals; a designated open workshop permits direct
experimentation under its source-authored law. Neither grants management authority
over unrelated shared objects, and deliberate management lockout is permitted.

## Current capability

DelveTalk uses its editable [Bend edition](docs/design/BEND.md) and one current source
receiving host. Lean owns semantics and admission; physical adapters provide custody,
transport and process I/O. Source-owned laws, guards, invariants, allocations and
multiobject transactions preserve current authority and exact preimages. Older tagged
DSL and executable host profiles are retired from the current production route.

The [living conversational document](docs/design/TOPLEVEL.md) joins prose, forms,
offered actions and local notation. Browser, authenticated HTTP and Town expose the
same source encounter. Account isolation, read-gated object/history acquisition,
bounded catalogue and indexed history have receiving checks. Exact receipt recovery
precedes current presentation/runtime validation, including uncertain session and
membership outcomes; retry does not regrant revoked authority.

Source [reflection](profiles/REFLECTION.md), editing, authored examples, compiler work
and governed adoption form one in-world workflow. A configured physical worker checks
source-selected work and retains attributed reports. Participants can revise owned
objects or propose shared changes, inspect examples and adopt changed behavior under
current law. SourceCustody handles source bytes; it does not duplicate this workflow.
Typed factory configuration and explicit exports support composed editable objects.

Source scenes, Commons, rooms, observed connections, causal messages and appointments
have focused receiving checks, including stale references, restart, terminal settlement
and atomic refusal. Multiobject authored examples use actual preceding typed results.
Garden and the collaborative constellation retain attribution and revisions.
Two-player [Automatafl](protocols/automatafl/README.md) preserves its original 11×11 rules,
private commitments and source-owned commit/reveal orchestration.

Interpretation policy, vocabulary and templates are authored in source. Captured forms
revise stored policy with attribution, invalidate stale encounters and affect the next
request. Controlled-provider account and authenticated HTTP journeys qualify custody,
admission and exact replay. The exact Haiku model is available, but bounded real
requests returned 429; successful real-provider completion remains unqualified and
further calls are stopped.

receiving caching reuses only exact checked artifacts, never authority or state/results.
A passive zero-method creation source/schema hash validation repair remains active.
The remaining evaluation repair connects native admitted Data directly to demand evaluation
without an AST roundtrip or output-cap increase. Scoped successful checks do not qualify
all later combinations. At most two local compiler seats use independent writable
snapshots; installation and hosted rehearsal require the exact joined closure.

## Boundaries

Mini supplies the attributed upstream baseline; DelveTalk's local edition owns its
current semantics. Programming preserves law. References, presence, possession and
presentation confer no authority. Revocation cannot unsend admitted messages; external
outbox intent does not establish delivery. Private custody, credentials, drafts and
participant transcripts stay outside Git. No external Delve, PDS or wiki publication
is authorized by repository work.
