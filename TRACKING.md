# DelveTalk status

**No v1 has launched. Hosted worlds are disposable previews.** Fresh seeds replace
previous previews; their roots and history need no migration. A running world's
exact admission, durable receipts and uncertain-reply recovery remain contracts.

## Implemented locally

These paths have focused receiving checks. **The full joined suite is not yet
complete; regression against a stable source snapshot is running.** Local results
establish neither deployment nor a general proof.

- [Resident storage](profiles/RESIDENT-STORE.md), daemon and consumers use native
  admission and incremental custody. [Messages and relay](docs/RESIDENT-ACTIVITY.md)
  retain provenance and consume atomically; disabled-slot handling and the
  four-slot emission budget are checked.
- Typed source objects, observation and Context2, law-held invariants, sealed
  modules, [object-local source tables](profiles/OBJECT-SOURCE-TABLES.md), and
  [receiving source contracts](protocols/source-contract/README.md) are implemented.
  [Reflection](profiles/REFLECTION.md) executes source specification metadata.
- [Editor](protocols/editor/README.md) generations g1–g6 cover repeated revision,
  drift, late compilation and recovery. [Containment](protocols/containment/README.md),
  [appointments](protocols/appointments/README.md), and the
  [resident library](protocols/resident-library/README.md) run as ordinary Bend.
- [Spween handlers](protocols/spween-handlers/README.md) have native execution,
  oracle comparison, views and [two-author adoption](protocols/spween-handler-workshop/README.md).
- Posts carry captured cards; manual interpretation and literal spells share
  receiving admission. Posts are the portal: participants need neither JSON nor
  a browser. Two-player [Automatafl](protocols/automatafl/README.md) retains the
  original 11×11 setup, private commitments and complete-match receiving checks.

## Boundaries

The independent DelveTalk host is intentional; Mini supplies language semantics
and selective reuse. Programming preserves law. References, presence, possession,
provenance and presentation grant no authority. Source revocation cannot unsend
ordinary admitted messages; external outbox intent does not establish delivery.

No external Delve, PDS or wiki publication is authorized. Private custody, credentials,
drafts and participant transcripts stay outside Git.
