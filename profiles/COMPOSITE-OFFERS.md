# Captured source preparation invitations

An ordinary Bend preparation export chooses a complete bounded turn, an authored question, or a refusal from explicit contribution data and captured observations. The shared contract is [Source preparation](PREPARATION.md). There is no client recipe language, field substitution table, or Python workflow interpreter.

Ordinary Town cards and Portal encounters discover the source view’s invitations. Custody retains the selected export and exact owner and observation roots; preparation does not refresh them. Native binding anchors those roots and validates structural effects. Current receiving law admits the resulting turn under the verified participant; an invitation grants no authority.

Questions and refusals retain their source outcome without submitting a world transaction. A ready turn uses the existing exact request, uncertain reply, and receipt custody. Manual speech interpretation retains the original prose and operator basis separately from contribution data.

The historical `delvetalk-composite-offer-v1` recipe format is retired. `scripts/composite_offers.py` is a transport name for the source invitation API. Receiving evidence lives in `conformance/test_preparation.py`, `test_town_preparation.py`, and the repeated Editor journey.
