# Source writing participants

`Writing.obend` is a reusable ordinary source participant configured with an
existing Candidate, target and syntax. Its submission invitation captures both
objects. `prepareSubmit` asks for missing source/examples or constructs one
native turn invoking Candidate.submit with the complete captured target state
as migration. It does not compile or grant adoption authority.

`WritingFactory.obend` allocates a configured participant from an explicitly
supplied checked Writing program. Source constructs its initial typed data,
child law and absent-child assumption; native allocation validates the checked
program/state and the parent quota. The factory's current law controls who may
make cards. Candidate submission and adoption remain governed separately.

The physical package loader seals named modules and evaluates constructors.
It does not rewrite source, generate behavior expressions or interpret plans.
Spween's handler workshop reuses these participants through normal Town cards.
